How to write the text
  • One printed line per line, its words in reading order. Lines are matched in any order when scored, so line order matters little; what is on each line matters.
  • Side-by-side columns are separate lines: the heading's left column (company, Số) first, then the right (national title, motto, place and date). A draft often joins them: split them.
  • Tables: one row per line, cell by cell, left to right: all of a cell's text (wrapped over several printed lines, as heading rows often are) before the next cell's. The draft reads across the whole row, a piece of each cell per line: regroup it. A word printed in three cells is written three times.
  • Repeats: keep what the page prints twice (a Vietnamese line and its English twin share names and numbers); delete what only the draft repeats.
  • Letters and marks as printed: â ê ô ơ ư ă đ are letters, so a draft's "e" for "ê" is a wrong letter. Where the scan lost a mark (faint, under a stamp), write the word if only one word fits; if unsure, your best reading, and say so in the note.
  • Leave out the signature box and handwritten signatures. Keep hand-filled numbers and dates, as a reader needs them. Under a stamp, type the printed letters you can read; a word you cannot read: type what you can, say so in the note.
